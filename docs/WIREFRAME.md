# Selective Solutions: landing page wireframe

Template mirrored: Cresta (IT Solutions & Technology HTML template), home 1.
Client: Selective Solutions LLC, Conyers, GA. IT support, VoIP phones, cybersecurity and backup for small and mid-size businesses.

## Strategy

**Audience.** Owners and office managers of 5 to 100 person businesses in Rockdale, Newton and nearby counties. They are not IT people. They buy when something breaks, when a vendor lets them down, or when a phone or ransomware scare makes them think about risk.

**The page's one job.** Get the visitor to call 770-929-1155 or send a meeting request.

**What sets them apart (all verified on selectivesolutions.com).**
- A live person answers the phone.
- Emergency response within one hour, guaranteed.
- In IT since 1995, incorporated in Georgia in 1999. Some clients have stayed more than 20 years.
- One vendor for computers, network, phones, email, backups and security. They also deal with the internet provider and copier/cloud vendors.
- Fixed monthly pricing with 24/7 monitoring.

**Objections to answer on the page.**
| Objection | Where it is answered |
|---|---|
| "Will anyone actually pick up?" | Hero, top bar, header, FAQ 1 |
| "What if we get hit by ransomware?" | Atlanta Cutlery story, backup service, FAQ 3 |
| "Switching phones means losing our numbers" | Phone service card, FAQ 2 |
| "IT costs are unpredictable" | Managed IT card, Why us row 2, FAQ 4 |
| "You're too small / too local for our other office" | FAQ 5, coverage ticker |

**Proof rules.** Only facts from the client's own site. Two real testimonials, quoted verbatim. Partner names from their Partners page, set as plain text (no borrowed logo files). No invented stats, team members, reviews, dates or awards.

## Visual system

- Colors: ink #071214, deep #0B1B1E, panel #11272B, signal yellow #FFC20E (from the logo), amber #F59A0B (gradient end, replaces Cresta's blue→violet), trace teal #4FD8C4 (from the video, status dots only), paper #F2F5F4.
- Type: Archivo (expanded width, 700–800) for headings; Public Sans for body; IBM Plex Mono for phone numbers, tags and labels.
- Signature: the phone line is treated as a live object. A pulsing "live person answers" indicator sits next to the number in the hero, header and contact. The coverage ticker is outlined Archivo, as in Cresta's text slider, but it lists real towns.
- Imagery: stills cut from the client's own hero video (no stock people, no invented photos). Swap in office or team photos when the client sends them.
