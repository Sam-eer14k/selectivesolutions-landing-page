6 questions
What happens when we have an emergency?

Call 770-929-1155. A live person answers, and we guarantee an emergency response within one hour. Often a technician can log in remotely and start working right away.

Can we keep our phone numbers if we switch phone systems?

Yes. Your existing numbers move to the new system. If you have more than one location, we link the systems so calls transfer between offices.

Can you recover a single deleted file?

Yes. We take full-image backups, check that they run on schedule and keep a 3-year history. You can get back one file or rebuild a whole computer.

As a fixed monthly cost, so IT becomes a line in your budget instead of a string of surprise bills. [CONFIRM: what the monthly price is based on, like number of computers or users]
